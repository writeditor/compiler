Abstract

We argue that scholarly articles should be authored directly in semantic HTML. Using RASH, a strict subset of HTML, papers become machine-readable by construction: every section, figure and citation is addressable, searchable and editable by both humans and agents.

Introduction

LaTeX produces beautiful PDFs, but the PDF is where structure goes to die. Search engines, reviewers and AI agents all need the structure back [1], [2].

Typesetting systems such as LaTeX [3] and the literate-programming tradition [4] showed that a document can be both readable and machine-processed; we bring that idea to the web.
